# SIRAP missing metric inputs — August 2026

This inventory covers the currently authoritative regional delivery packets: **Eje Cafetero at 300 m** and **Orinoquía at 500 m**. The listed metrics have the artifact status `blocked` while their underlying data or contract remains pending. They will remain blocked until the requested regional artifact or approved national contract is supplied and validated. National inputs must not be used as an unannounced substitute for a missing regional input.

The blockers are not all absent raster files. Some are delivered rasters whose scientific meaning is not documented sufficiently for the metric, while others are missing national denominators or metadata contracts. The supplied Orinoquía Magnoliopsida RDS files passed full summary parity and are therefore **not** a missing plant-matrix blocker.
