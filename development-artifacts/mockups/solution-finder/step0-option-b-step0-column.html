Design Concern

  • Horizontal compression: Steps 2 and 3 are squeezed to col-span-2 (~160-200px at 1024px). Card labels truncate and descriptions are barely readable.
  • Wasted space: The Step 0 column has only 2 radio options — lots of empty vertical space below the scope card.
  • Step renumbering: "Step 0" is unusual and breaks the established 1-2-3 mental model, requiring cognitive adjustment.
  • Mobile breakpoint: 5 stacked columns on mobile is significantly longer than the current 4.
