Map Impact

Fill and border answer different map-reading questions.

Why split fill and border?

Fill communicates coverage. Border communicates extent or administrative edge, especially when fills overlap.

Fill: blue hatch
Border: dark dash

Recommendation

Let Fill and Border chips open the appearance popover.

The expanded row keeps opacity, fill state, and border state visible. Clicking either chip opens the same detailed popover, so there is no redundant third Appearance button.

Default visible controls

  • Opacity slider and percent stay visible.
  • Fill chip shows color plus fill pattern and opens the popover.
  • Border chip shows stroke color and line style and opens the popover.
  • No separate Appearance button is needed in the row.

Popover contents

Fill owns color, pattern, and density. Border owns color, style, and width. This keeps the mental model explicit without increasing every layer row's default height.

Trigger choice

The Fill and Border chips act like editable labels: they preview current styling, then open the full Fill and Border editor when clicked.
