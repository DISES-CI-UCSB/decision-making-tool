Variant 5

Stacked entry card

No tabs. No "Login vs. Register" debate on the first screen. Just three clear choices on a single card: Google, email-password, or request access. The email form expands inline when chosen. This is the pattern you see on modern SaaS landing pages (Linear, Notion, Vercel) and it's the friendliest on cognitive load.

Tradeoffs · Pros: Lowest cognitive load — three explicit doors instead of a tab switcher; "Request access" visually distinct from sign-in (dashed border) so returning users don't hesitate; aligns with modern SaaS convention. Cons: Multi-step (entry → email form) means one extra click for password users; back-button needs clear affordance.

Best fit: if we want to bias users toward Google SSO (faster, no password hell) and clearly separate "I have an account" from "I want an account" without making them parse a tab UI.
