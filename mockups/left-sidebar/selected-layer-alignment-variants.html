Selected-layer card — 5 alignment variants

The current card has four right-side elements — eye icon, caret, "70%", color swatch — that each live at a slightly different x-position. Each variant below explores a different combination of: (1) eye-icon box on/off, (2) 70% inline with the slider vs. its own column, and (3) eye-to-caret gap. All variants use the same grid-template-rows collapse animation (240ms cubic-bezier). Click a card header to toggle its expanded/collapsed state and feel the transition.

Tip: click a row's caret to toggle just that card.

Variant 1

Ghost eye, unified right column

Eye and caret become slim icon-only buttons (w-5). Color swatch wrapper is also w-5. All three rightmost elements share one 20 px column. "70%" stays in its own right-aligned column.

Variant 2

Bordered eye + 70% inline

Keeps the bordered eye as the primary affordance. "70%" sits right after the slider thumb (no separate column). Eye + caret hugged at gap-1 (4 px) so the eye pulls toward the caret, with the Color label + swatch mirroring the same tightened gap below. Caret still w-5.

My pick

Variant 3 — Matched w-7 column

Strongest alignment (all right-side hit targets in one column), keeps the bordered eye as the primary action, and "70%" inline reads as live slider feedback instead of a separate column. Only real cost is that the caret looks visually equal to the eye; mitigated with a slightly lighter chevron color.

Runner-up: Variant 2 if you want to keep the caret visually lighter than the eye.
